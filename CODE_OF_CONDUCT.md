# Code of conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Discuss the work, explain disagreements, and make room for people with different levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, and sharing someone's private information without consent are unacceptable. This applies in project issues, pull requests, and other project discussions.

Report problems privately through the [maintainer's contact page](https://youssef.tn/#contact). The maintainer may remove content or restrict participation based on the behavior and its impact.
