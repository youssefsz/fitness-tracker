Privacy policy

How Fitness Tracker handles data in the app and on this website.

Data in the app

Fitness Tracker works offline and has no account or server for your workouts. The app stores exercise entries, goals, settings, and any profile details, photo, or weigh-ins you add in its local database. The photo picker lets you choose an optional profile photo.

Exports and AI imports

A JSON backup can include workouts, goals, settings, profile details, and weigh-ins. It excludes the profile photo. CSV exports contain workout sets and notes. You choose where exported files go and who can access them.

Import from AI chat can copy a prompt to your clipboard. If you opt in, that prompt includes exercise names and units. Any text you paste into another service is handled under that service's policy. Fitness Tracker reads the JSON you bring back on your device.

Delete your records

Use Profile → Your data → Reset all app data to clear records, settings, and local recovery backups from the app. Manage any copies you exported or shared in the location where you saved them.

Website and downloads

This site saves your chosen appearance in your browser. Cloudflare serves the website and handles requests to deliver and protect it. GitHub hosts the APKs and release notes and handles requests when you visit GitHub or download an APK. See the Cloudflare privacy policy and GitHub privacy statement.

Contact

Contact Youssef Dhibi with privacy questions. Keep personal workout data out of public issues.
