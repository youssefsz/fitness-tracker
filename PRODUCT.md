## Platform

adaptive

## Stack

Flutter and Dart for iOS, iPadOS, and Android.

## Purpose

V records completed exercise sets and shows how personal performance changes over time. People can log reps or timed efforts, correct dates and values, and set an optional best-set target.

## Constraints

The app works offline with no account. Overview only reflects recorded activity. The latest set, best set, and daily volume answer different questions. Users can move their records with a JSON backup or export sets for analysis as CSV. The three primary destinations and the floating navigation come from the supplied design kit.
